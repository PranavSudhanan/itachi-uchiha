Voice lines

The .wav files here are Itachi's call-outs, generated with Microsoft's Keita Neural Japanese male voice
(tools/voice-lines.py, calm delivery, pitch -8 Hz) and then deepened with Web Audio: played at 0.88x
(lower pitch and chest resonance, calm pace), high-pass 60 Hz, low shelf +4 dB at 220 Hz, -2.5 dB at 2.6 kHz,
+1.5 dB at 5.2 kHz, soft tanh saturation, 7% short room reverb, light compression, normalised to -1 dBFS.
Median pitch ends up around 100 Hz. To replace a line, put audio you have the rights to use here, named after the line. Supported: .mp3 .ogg .m4a .wav
When a file is present it plays instead of the browser's speech engine (subtitles still show).
